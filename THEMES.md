## Gradual awareness: four levels of realisation (nik, 2026-11-07)

A framework for the love and bhakti part, building on "we are spirit soul, a part of the Divine".

1. **Body, mind and awareness.** Tell apart the body, the mind, and the deeper awareness or consciousness, with a Buddhist-style approach and vocabulary. How the mind works and connects to the body. Imprints can be stored in the brain, in the body, and in the mental or subtle body. Self-awareness gets covered or merged when we identify with body, mind and the patterns we create or absorb from our environment. How we free ourselves from those patterns and realise we are awareness, not the patterns.
2. **Brahman (sat).** What that awareness is: eternity, spirit, the nature of consciousness. God as Brahman; self-realisation in Brahman.
3. **Paramātmā (sat-cit).** Love begins: the individual consciousness and the super-consciousness. The soul takes shelter in its link with the Paramātmā. A meditative, yogic love, like the yogīs who meditate on the Paramātmā. Explore this concept further.
4. **Bhagavān (sat-cit-ānanda).** The Supreme, or Parabrahman, with nāma, guṇa, rūpa and līlā (names, qualities, forms and activities), and how the soul experiences ānanda.

Threads running through it:
- Map how different traditions put their emphasis on one of the four levels. All of them speak of the same reality, in more or less detail.
- Don't skip steps. Higher levels need grounding and realisation in the earlier ones. No one can claim advanced bhakti on the Bhagavān level without progress on the levels before it. In this sense everything is harmonised.
- Tie-in to pitfalls (theme 3) and karma (theme 2): mental imprints shape consciousness and actions, and together they create consequences and experiences for the mind.
