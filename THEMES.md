# Future themes (backlog)

2. **What is karma?** The process by which this world brings the best out of us. It teaches us to love without motivation and without interruption.
3. **Pitfalls on the path of bhakti.** What diminishes bhakti and what enhances it, and the consequences: the processes of elevation and degradation.
4. **Bhakti and modern psychology (advanced).** For example, traumas as vāsanās, imprints on the subtle body. How the bhakti process acts on the root of karmic seeds and vāsanās and purifies the heart of anarthas.
5. **Kīrtan and the holy name.** Its central place in bhakti practice, as recommended for this age.
