Themes

A path in six steps

These themes build on one another, from what bhakti is to how it is practised. You can read them in any order, but they make the most sense in this one.

  1. What is bhakti?

    Love as the soul's eternal nature: covered, but never lost.

  2. What is karma?

    How the world brings out the best in us and teaches us to love without motive and without interruption.

  3. Four levels of awareness

    From body and mind, to Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān.

  4. Pitfalls on the path

    What weakens love and what nourishes it, the three guṇas, and how we fall and rise.

  5. Healing at the root

    Bhakti and modern psychology on vāsanās, trauma and anarthas.

  6. Kīrtan and the holy name

    Calling on God across traditions, and the path given for this age.

The Spiritual Unity Movement offers this site as its own perspective, with respect for every tradition it quotes.
